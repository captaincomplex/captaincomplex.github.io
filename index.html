Ident

Flight-duty board · e-paper & LED matrix

A wall-mounted board that reads a pilot's roster and keeps itself up to date — today's sectors, report time and first departure, plus the next duty on days off. Route maps are drawn on the device from a bundled coastline. Thirteen display styles.

Built for the household as much as the pilot: one glance tells you when they're flying and when they're home.

Spotipi + Photo

64×64 LED matrix · Raspberry Pi

An LED panel that cycles photos from an Apple Photos album and switches to Spotify cover art the moment music starts. A web UI controls modes, live brightness, photo interval, a screen timer and a daily schedule — and deleting a photo from the album removes it from the panel.

Twitter Offline

Progressive web app · iPhone

Reads your X feed with no connection at all — full tweet text, photos and video, cached on the device. A bookmarklet captures the feed using your own logged-in session; the app stores it locally, so it opens and scrolls with zero network.
