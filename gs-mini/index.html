Ground Speed Mini

On final approach in managed speed, an Airbus does not simply fly VAPP. It flies whatever indicated airspeed keeps its ground speed from dropping below a floor called GS mini. The magenta speed target moves with the wind, so the aircraft carries extra energy into gusts and keeps it when a headwind suddenly dies.

The idea in one sentence

The tower wind you enter on the MCDU PERF APPR page tells the computer what headwind to expect at the runway. From that, it works out the lowest ground speed you should ever see on approach. If the headwind aloft is stronger than at the runway, the aircraft must fly faster through the air to keep that ground speed. When the extra headwind disappears near the ground, the aircraft's inertia keeps its ground speed, and the airspeed falls back to VAPP instead of below it.

APPR COR  = ⅓ × tower headwind, min 5, max 15 kt
VAPP      = VLS + APPR COR
GS mini   = VAPP − tower headwind
IAS target = max( VAPP , GS mini + current headwind )  A320ceo, k = 1
A320neo, k = 0.33: IAS target = max( VAPP , VAPP + 0.33 × (current headwind − tower headwind) )

Why it matters: a headwind that dies on short final

An aircraft's ground speed cannot change instantly. When the headwind drops by 20 kt in a few seconds, indicated airspeed drops by roughly 20 kt at once, and autothrust needs time to win it back. Tower wind is 20 kt headwind and VLS is 128 kt, so VAPP is 135 kt and GS mini is 115 kt.

Rules of the function

  • Managed speed onlyGS mini drives the target only when speed is managed and the approach phase is active. With a selected speed, the aircraft flies exactly what you dialled.
  • Never below VAPPThe target has a floor of VAPP. GS mini can only add speed, never take it away.
  • Headwind components onlyTailwind is treated as zero headwind, both for the tower wind and the current wind. With a tower tailwind, GS mini equals VAPP.
  • Capped by flap limitsThe target stays below VFE of the current configuration, so a strong wind aloft cannot drive it into a flap overspeed.
  • Garbage in, garbage outThe tower wind on PERF APPR sets GS mini. Enter the latest ATIS or tower wind; an exaggerated headwind lowers GS mini and removes the protection.
  • A320ceo and A320neo differThe extra headwind is added with a factor k: 1 on the A320ceo, 0.33 on the A320neo and the other Airbus types, whose lower deceleration capability could otherwise leave too much speed at the flare. Switch between them with the Aircraft control.
  • Watch it moveIn gusty conditions the magenta triangle walks up and down the speed tape. That is normal and is the function working.
