Before you push

Objective: get from FL380 to stable at 1,000 ft on the glideslope: gear down, flaps 3 or FULL, below VAPP + 30, no overspeed.

  • Highest score: one continuous descent at idle thrust, all the way to glideslope capture. Levelling off adds thrust and costs the PERFECT star.
  • Gates: about 3 NM per 1,000 ft. FL100 by about 40 NM, slowing to 250 kt; 3,000 ft and slowing by about 10 NM. Add miles for a tailwind.
  • Stay “on the glide” (inside the green cone) and you are on a sensible path. The glideslope captures itself.
  • Speedbrake: drag grows with speed squared, so it is strongest when fast. Not available once the gear is down.
  • Gear: lots of drag, below 250 kt only. Flaps: each stage has a speed limit, and you can't slow below clean minimum speed without them.
  • Anti-ice: switch it on in icing cloud or ice builds and costs stars. It raises idle thrust, so you descend more shallowly.
  • Landing lights: a tiny bit of drag. Mostly for morale.
  • Hazards: fly into a thunderstorm and it's over; get close and you lose points. Same near the mountains (keep 2,000 ft clear). TCAS traffic: level off on a TA or the RA flies itself and costs a star.
